# World Knowledge: City of Midgaard (Updated for Test 5)

Updated: 2026-07-18
Last updated by: Test 5 checkpoint

## Sewer System (Explored in Test 5)

### Confirmed Sewer Rooms and Exits

#### The Watery Sewer
Status: Confirmed by travel
Description: Water up to hips; can barely see anything.
Exits: north, south

#### The Watery Sewer Bend
Status: Confirmed by travel from The Watery Sewer going south
Description: Still water-filled sewer that bends. Exits: north (back to Watery Sewer), west (to Dark Passageway)

#### The Dark Passageway
Status: Confirmed by travel from Watery Sewer Bend going west
Description: Dry ground; water floor visible east through arched entry leading back to watery sewer. Exits: north, east (returns to Watery Sewer Bend), west

#### The Junction
Status: Confirmed by travel from Dark Passageway going west
Description: Pipe junction with multiple exits. Exits: north, east (returns to Dark Passageway), west

#### The Junction Going Three Ways
Status: Confirmed by travel from Junction going west
Description: Passageway in the pipes of the sewer system. Previously contained a huge hungry-looking sewer rat (now gone). Exits: north, east (returns to Junction), west

#### The Sewer Entrance (current)
Status: Confirmed by travel from Junction Going Three Ways going north
Description: Standing in middle of pipe leading from north to south. Above is an air shaft leading up into sunlight (impossible to climb that way). Exits: north (unexplored), south (returns to Junction Going Three Ways)
